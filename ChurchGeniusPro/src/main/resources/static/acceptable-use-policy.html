Acceptable Use Policy

Protecting churches, members, and the ChurchGeniusPro platform.

Effective Date: June 21, 2026 Last Updated: June 21, 2026 Version: 1.0

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of the ChurchGeniusPro platform (the "Service") and is part of, and incorporated into, our Terms of Service. It applies to all users, including churches, administrators, staff, volunteers, members, and any other authorized users. The purpose of this AUP is to protect the security, integrity, and availability of the Service and the privacy and safety of all who rely on it.

1. Authorized Use

You may use the Service only for lawful church-administration purposes, in accordance with your subscription, your assigned role and permissions, the Terms of Service, and this AUP. Access is limited to authorized users acting within the scope of their permissions and the legitimate needs of the church they serve.

2. Prohibited Activities

You must not use the Service to engage in any activity that is unlawful, harmful, or that interferes with others. The following sections describe categories of prohibited conduct. These examples are illustrative, not exhaustive.

3. Unauthorized Access Attempts

You must not attempt to gain unauthorized access to any part of the Service, other users' accounts, organizations' data, or our systems or networks. This includes probing, scanning, or testing the vulnerability of the Service; circumventing authentication, access controls, role or permission restrictions, or network-based access restrictions; using another user's credentials without authorization; or accessing data not intended for you.

4. Malware & Harmful Content

You must not upload, transmit, or distribute any virus, worm, malware, ransomware, or other malicious or harmful code, or any file or content designed to disrupt, damage, disable, or gain unauthorized access to any system, data, or device. You must not use the Service to facilitate attacks against any third party.

5. Spam, Phishing & Fraud

You must not use the Service to send spam or unsolicited bulk communications, to conduct phishing or social-engineering attacks, to impersonate any person or organization, to misrepresent your identity or affiliation, or to engage in any fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading activity. Communications sent through the Service must comply with applicable anti-spam and consent laws, and recipients must have a lawful basis to be contacted.

6. Illegal, Offensive & Infringing Content

You must not upload, store, or share content that is illegal, that infringes the intellectual property or other rights of any party without permission, that is defamatory, harassing, threatening, obscene, or hateful, or that exploits or endangers minors. You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights and any necessary licenses or consents for all content you place in the Service.

7. Misuse of Member Data

Member and personal information in the Service must be handled responsibly and only for legitimate church purposes. You must not access, use, export, sell, or disclose member data beyond what your role and the church's purposes require, and you must not use the Service to surveil, harass, or harm any individual. Administrators must grant access on a need-to-know basis and respect privacy settings configured within the Service.

8. Security & Password Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account. You must use strong, unique passwords; keep credentials confidential; not share accounts; and sign out of shared devices. You must promptly report any suspected compromise. Administrators are responsible for managing user accounts, deactivating access when users leave or change roles, and supervising any temporary or device-based access mechanisms.

9. System Abuse & Resource Consumption

You must not abuse, overburden, or interfere with the Service or its infrastructure. Prohibited conduct includes excessive or automated requests that degrade performance, scraping or harvesting data, using bots or scripts not expressly permitted, attempting to bypass usage limits, and any activity that imposes an unreasonable or disproportionate load on our systems. AI-assisted and other resource-intensive features must be used reasonably and in line with any applicable usage limits.

10. Violations & Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations of this AUP and cooperate with law-enforcement authorities where appropriate. In response to a violation, we may take actions including issuing a warning, removing or disabling content, restricting or revoking access or permissions, throttling or limiting resource usage, and suspending or terminating accounts. We may act immediately and without prior notice where a violation poses a risk to security, other users, or the platform.

11. Suspension & Termination Procedures

Where practical, we will notify the affected organization's administrator of a violation and provide an opportunity to remedy it. For serious or repeated violations, or where required to protect the Service or others, we may suspend or terminate access without prior notice. Suspension or termination for violations does not entitle you to a refund, and you remain responsible for fees accrued. Reinstatement, if any, is at our discretion once the violation is resolved.

12. Reporting Abuse or Security Concerns

If you become aware of a violation of this AUP, a security vulnerability, or suspicious activity, please report it promptly.

Email support@churchgeniuspro.com with details. Please do not publicly disclose a suspected vulnerability before we have had a reasonable opportunity to address it.
