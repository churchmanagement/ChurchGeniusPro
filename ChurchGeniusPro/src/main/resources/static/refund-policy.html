It is part of, and incorporated into, our Terms of Service. By purchasing a subscription, you agree to this Policy. Our goal is to set clear expectations and minimize disputes.

1. Subscription Plans & Billing Terms

The Service is offered through paid subscription plans, which may include monthly and annual options and optional add-ons. The features, limits, and prices for each plan are presented at the time of purchase. Fees are stated in U.S. dollars and are exclusive of any applicable taxes, which may be added. By providing a payment method, you authorize us and our payment processor to charge the applicable fees and taxes to that method according to your selected plan and billing cycle.

2. Trial Periods

We may offer a free trial period so you can evaluate the Service. Unless stated otherwise at sign-up, no charge is made during the trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription will automatically begin and your payment method will be charged for the first billing cycle at the then-current rate. You may cancel at any time during the trial to avoid being charged. Trials are for evaluation and may be limited to one per organization.

3. Monthly & Annual Subscriptions

Monthly plans are billed in advance each month and renew monthly. Annual plans are billed in advance for the full year and renew annually, and are typically offered at a discount compared with paying monthly. Your billing cycle begins on the date your paid subscription starts (including immediately after a trial ends) and renews on the corresponding date each cycle.

4. Automatic Renewal

To prevent interruption, subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle at the then-current rate, until cancelled. We will charge your payment method on file at each renewal. If we change pricing, we will provide advance notice, and the new price will apply at your next renewal. You can avoid renewal by cancelling before the renewal date as described below.

5. Refund Eligibility

Because a free trial is available to evaluate the Service before payment, fees are generally non-refundable. We may, in our discretion or where required by law, provide a refund or credit in limited circumstances, such as:

  • A duplicate or clearly erroneous charge;
  • A documented, prolonged failure of the Service that we are unable to remedy and that is not excused under Section 10; or
  • A first-time annual subscription cancelled within fourteen (14) days of the initial charge where the Service has not been substantially used, subject to our review.

Approved refunds are calculated based on the unused portion of the current term, less any non-refundable amounts. Monthly fees already incurred are generally not refundable.

6. Non-Refundable Fees

Unless required by law or expressly stated otherwise, the following are non-refundable: fees for billing cycles already begun; partial-period usage; one-time setup, onboarding, or add-on fees; usage-based charges (such as message or AI-feature usage already consumed); and amounts owed for a cycle in which an account is suspended or terminated for violating our Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy.

7. Cancellation Procedures

You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings or by contacting support@churchgeniuspro.com. Cancellation stops future renewals. When you cancel, you will generally retain access to paid features until the end of your current paid billing cycle, after which the subscription will not renew. Cancelling does not, by itself, generate a refund for the current cycle except as provided in Section 5.

8. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, email support@churchgeniuspro.com from the email associated with your account and include your church name, the charge date and amount, and the reason for the request. We may ask for additional information to verify the account and evaluate the request. We review each request individually and will notify you of our decision.

9. Processing Timelines

We aim to review refund requests within a reasonable time, typically within five (5) to ten (10) business days. If a refund is approved, it will be issued to the original payment method. The time for the refunded amount to appear depends on your payment provider and is typically an additional five (5) to ten (10) business days after we process it.

10. Service Interruptions & Exceptions

Occasional, brief, or scheduled interruptions — including maintenance, updates, and outages caused by third-party providers or events beyond our reasonable control — are a normal part of any online service and do not, by themselves, entitle you to a refund or credit. We will use reasonable efforts to restore the Service promptly. Any service-level commitments, where offered, are stated in your applicable plan or order.

11. Chargeback Handling

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first so we can resolve it quickly. Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute without first contacting us may result in suspension of your account while the dispute is investigated. We will respond to chargebacks with relevant transaction and usage records. If a chargeback is found to be invalid, we may reinstate the corresponding fees and any associated costs, and we reserve the right to decline future service.
