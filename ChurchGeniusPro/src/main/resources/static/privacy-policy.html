Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect, and share information.

Effective Date: June 21, 2026 Last Updated: June 21, 2026 Version: 1.0

ChurchGeniusPro ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the ChurchGeniusPro platform (the "Service"), how we use and protect it, and the choices available to you. It applies to churches and organizations that subscribe to the Service, their administrators, staff, volunteers, and members, and visitors to public pages we host on their behalf. This Policy is designed to align with applicable U.S. privacy regulations and industry best practices.

Roles matter. For most member information, the church is the "controller" that decides what to collect and why, and ChurchGeniusPro is the "processor/service provider" that handles that information on the church's behalf and under its instructions. If you are a church member with questions about your data, please contact your church directly; we will assist your church in responding.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you and your organization provide to us, information generated automatically when the Service is used, and information from third-party providers that support the Service. The categories below describe what we collect and why.

2. Personal Information from Users

When an administrator, staff member, volunteer, or member creates or uses an account, we may collect: name; email address; phone number; username and securely hashed password; role and permission assignments; profile details; and records of acceptance of our legal policies, including timestamps and version information. We also collect information you provide when you contact support or communicate with us.

3. Church / Organization Information

When a church registers, we collect organizational details such as church name, mailing address, contact email and phone, tax/identification numbers (for example an EIN) where provided, website and social media links, logo, and configuration and subscription information. Billing and payment details are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full payment card numbers on our systems.

4. Member Information Stored by Churches

Churches use the Service to manage information about their members, families, children, visitors, and other contacts. Depending on how a church configures the Service, this may include names and nicknames, contact details, addresses, family relationships, birth and anniversary dates, attendance and check-in/check-out records, contributions and giving history, ministry and group assignments, notes, uploaded documents and images, and other records the church chooses to store. Some of this information may be marked private by the church.

This information is entered and controlled by the church. We process it on the church's behalf to provide the Service and do not use it for our own purposes except as described in this Policy. Churches are responsible for obtaining any required consents from the individuals whose information they store.

5. Usage & Analytics Information

We collect information about how the Service is accessed and used, such as features used, pages viewed, actions taken, audit and activity logs, dates and times of access, and error and diagnostic data. We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service. Where AI-assisted features are used, we may process the content you submit to those features to return a result and to monitor usage and cost.

6. Device & Browser Information

When you use the Service, we automatically collect technical information such as IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, language settings, and referring pages. This information helps us maintain security, prevent fraud, remember preferences, and understand how the Service performs across devices.

7. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service and its features;
  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users;
  • Process subscriptions, billing, and renewals;
  • Send transactional and administrative communications, and, where configured, reminders and notifications on behalf of churches;
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries;
  • Monitor, secure, and protect the Service against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
  • Maintain audit logs and records of policy acceptance for compliance;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use member information stored by churches for advertising.

8. How We Protect Information

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including encryption in transit, hashed storage of passwords, role-based access controls, tenant isolation so each organization's data is segregated, network-based access restrictions where configured, and activity logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for safeguarding your credentials and configuring access appropriately.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service to your organization, to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When a church's subscription ends, we make Customer Data available for export for a limited period and then delete or anonymize it in accordance with our retention practices, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Audit and acceptance records may be retained for compliance purposes.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of certain U.S. states may have additional rights, including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights.

If your information is stored by a church through the Service, please direct your request to that church, which controls the information; we will support the church in fulfilling valid requests. For information we control directly (such as account or billing data), contact us at support@churchgeniuspro.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and may need to verify your identity before acting.

11. Third-Party Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party providers who perform services on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, payment processing, email and SMS delivery, mapping and notifications, and artificial-intelligence processing for features like voice entry, document scanning, and assistance. These providers are permitted to use information only to perform services for us and are bound by confidentiality and security obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.

12. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Service, keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand usage. For details about the cookies we use and how to manage them, see our Cookie Policy.

13. International Data Transfers

The Service is operated from the United States, and information is processed and stored in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country. Where required, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

14. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for use by churches and their authorized administrators and is not directed to children. Churches may, however, store information about minors (for example, in kids ministry records) as part of their own operations. When they do, the church acts as the controller and is responsible for obtaining any consents required by law, such as parental or guardian consent. We process children's information only on the church's behalf to provide the Service and apply the same safeguards described in this Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for our own purposes.

15. Data Breach Notification

We maintain procedures to detect, investigate, and respond to security incidents. If we become aware of a data breach affecting personal information we process, we will notify affected organizations without undue delay and provide information to help them meet their own notification obligations, consistent with applicable law. Churches are responsible for notifying their members and any regulators where required.

16. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

ChurchGeniusPro — Privacy

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email support@churchgeniuspro.com.

17. Policy Updates & Revisions

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, the Service, or the law. When we make material changes, we will revise the "Last Updated" date and version above and may provide additional notice. The current version is always available at /privacy-policy. We maintain version records and, where acceptance is required, capture acceptance timestamps for audit purposes.
