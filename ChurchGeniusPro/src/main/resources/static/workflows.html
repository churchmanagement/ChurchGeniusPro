⚙️ Automations
When something happens in your church — a visitor checks in, a member slips to At risk, a follow-up goes overdue, a large gift arrives — a rule can create a follow-up, notify or e-mail a staff member, add a family note or draft an e-mail. Every rule is off until you switch it on, runs with your permissions, and never messages members.
