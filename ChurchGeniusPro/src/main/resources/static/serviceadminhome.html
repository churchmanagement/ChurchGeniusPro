Runs daily at 7:30 AM (Central) for paid-plan clients 10, 3 and 0 days before their next billing date. Both switches are off by default and work independently. The existing subscription expiry notices are not affected.

Churches can pay a sent invoice online on its secure invoice page — by card, ACH bank debit, bank transfer or any other method you enable in the Stripe Dashboard (Settings → Payment methods; nothing to change here). This uses the ChurchGeniusPro billing Stripe account, which is separate from every church's own donation Stripe account. The keys are set only in Azure App Service settings (PLATFORM_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, PLATFORM_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY, PLATFORM_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET) and are never stored in the database or shown here in full.

Test connection only reads the account (Stripe "balance" call); it changes nothing. In the Stripe dashboard, add a webhook endpoint for the events payment_intent.succeeded, payment_intent.payment_failed and payment_intent.partially_funded, and put its signing secret in PLATFORM_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET. Card payments are recorded immediately; ACH debit and bank transfer payments settle days later and are recorded only by the webhook. Bank transfer also needs the key to be allowed to create Customers (a restricted key must have Customers: write). To keep a Dashboard-enabled method off platform invoices, list it in PLATFORM_STRIPE_EXCLUDED_METHODS.
