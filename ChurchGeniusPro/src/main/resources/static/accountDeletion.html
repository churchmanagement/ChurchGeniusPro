Delete your ChurchGeniusPro account

For members who sign in to a church's Member Portal with the ChurchGeniusPro website or app.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to the Member Portal with your username and password.
  2. Open ⚙️ Settings (top right) and choose Delete my account…
  3. Enter your password, tick the confirmation box and press Delete my account.

Deletion takes effect immediately: you are signed out on every device and your login can no longer be used. A confirmation is emailed to the address on your member record.

Sign in to delete your account

What is deleted

  • Your Member Portal login (username and password), remembered devices and notification subscriptions.
  • Your portal preferences, favourites and the list of members you blocked.
  • Your Members Chat inbox. Messages you sent remain in the other person's inbox, shown as from "Former member".

What your church keeps

Your church remains the keeper of its own membership record, giving history and year-end statements. Churches are required to keep those for their accounting and tax reporting, so deleting your login does not remove them. To be removed from the church's records as well, please contact the church office directly — only the church can make that change.

Can't sign in?

If you no longer have access to your login, ask your church office to delete it for you from Users, or write to support@churchgeniuspro.com from the email address on your member record.

Staff, church and child accounts

Staff and church-owner logins are managed by the church administrator. Child logins in the Kids Portal are managed by the church and the child's family; they cannot be deleted from inside the portal.
