1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences and sign-in state, and to provide information to site operators. "Similar technologies" include local storage, session storage, and pixels, which serve comparable purposes. We refer to all of these collectively as "cookies" in this Policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to operate and secure the Service, to keep you signed in, to remember your settings, and to understand how the Service is used so we can improve it. We group the cookies we use into the categories described below.

3. Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They enable core functionality such as page navigation, secure areas, and form submission. Without these cookies, the Service cannot be provided. Because they are essential, they do not require consent.

4. Authentication & Session Cookies

We use session cookies to identify you when you sign in and to keep you authenticated as you move between pages, so you do not have to log in repeatedly. These cookies are essential to providing a secure, logged-in experience and are typically deleted when you close your browser or sign out. "Remember me" functionality, where offered, may use a longer-lived cookie to keep you signed in across sessions.

5. Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember choices you make to personalize your experience, such as display settings, recently used options, and similar preferences. They make the Service more convenient but are not strictly necessary for it to function.

6. Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how the Service is used — for example, which features are used and how the Service performs — so we can improve reliability and usability. The information is used in aggregate and is not used to serve advertising. Where required, we use analytics cookies only with your consent.

7. Security-Related Cookies

Security cookies help us detect and prevent fraudulent activity, protect against unauthorized access, and maintain the integrity of the Service. They support functions such as session protection and abuse prevention and are part of keeping your account and data safe.

8. Third-Party Cookies

Some features rely on third-party providers — such as payment processors, communications providers, mapping or notification services, and analytics — which may set their own cookies when their functionality is used. These cookies are governed by the third party's own policies. We do not control third-party cookies and encourage you to review the relevant provider's cookie and privacy notices.

9. Cookie Consent Management

Where required by law, we present a cookie consent banner the first time you visit. Strictly necessary cookies are always active because the Service cannot operate without them. For non-essential categories, such as analytics and preferences, you may accept or decline. Your choice is remembered so we do not repeatedly prompt you, and you can change your choice at any time by clearing the consent and revisiting the Service, or by using your browser controls described below.

10. Managing or Disabling Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, and block cookies through their settings. The steps vary by browser; consult your browser's help resources for instructions. You can typically:

  • Delete existing cookies stored on your device;
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies;
  • Receive a warning before a cookie is stored; and
  • Use private/incognito browsing, which limits cookie persistence.

You can also decline non-essential cookies through our consent banner where it is presented.

11. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you block or delete essential, authentication, or security cookies, parts of the Service may not function properly — for example, you may be unable to sign in or stay signed in, and certain features may be unavailable. Disabling preference or analytics cookies has a smaller impact: the Service will still work, but it may be less personalized, and we will have less information to improve it.

12. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, the Service, or the law. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date and version above. The current version is always available at /cookie-policy. Questions? Email support@churchgeniuspro.com.
