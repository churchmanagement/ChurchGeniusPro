Delete my login

Deletes your staff login straight away:

  • you can no longer sign in, and every device you are signed in on is signed out;
  • your e-mail, phone and address are removed from your staff record, with your permissions, access tokens, push notifications and AI data-sharing answers.

Records you entered for the church (for example gifts, expenses or follow-ups) stay in the church's history with your name, because the church needs them for its accounts.

Delete the whole church account

Deletes the church and all of its records: members, families, giving, events, attendance, messages, files and every login.

  • We e-mail a confirmation link to the church's registered address. Nothing happens until that link is confirmed.
  • After confirmation the church is deleted within 30 days and you get an e-mail when it is done. You keep full access until then — export what you need first, for example from the Family & Member Report or Accounting Reports.
  • ChurchGenius LLC keeps only its own invoices and payment records for your subscription (tax records). Copies in database backups are removed as those backups expire.

Deleting your account

A login that is not a staff or church login cannot be deleted here. Member Portal logins: open Settings in the Member Portal and choose Delete my account. Other questions: support@churchgeniuspro.com.
