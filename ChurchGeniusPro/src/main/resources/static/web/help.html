Frequently Asked Questions

Is my church's data secure?
Yes. Every church's data is isolated in a multi-tenant architecture with role-based access control, encrypted sensitive fields, audited temporary access, and automatic backups. Optional protections include NFC-tag login and locking sensitive pages to your building's WiFi network.
Can you migrate our data from our current system?
Yes — Standard and Pro plans include free migration support. We import members, families, groups, income, and expenses from CSV exports of most church management systems.
Do volunteers need training to use it?
Most volunteers are productive in minutes. Check-in, attendance, and event screens are designed for Sunday-morning simplicity, and the built-in AI assistant answers "how do I…" questions in plain English.
How does online giving work?
Each church connects its own payment account and receives gifts directly. Donors give from any device; gifts post automatically to your accounting with fund designations and statements.
What does the AI integration actually do?
Search your data in plain English ("show families that joined this year"), use voice commands to record income or expenses, and scan checks or bank statements to enter transactions automatically.
Can we cancel or change plans anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel whenever you need — your data remains exportable.
Is there a mobile app?
ChurchGeniusPro is an installable progressive web app — add it to the home screen on iOS or Android for a full-screen app experience with push notifications.

Still have questions?

Our team responds quickly — usually the same business day.
