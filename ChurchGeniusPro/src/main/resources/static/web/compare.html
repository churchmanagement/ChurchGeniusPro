The honest summary

Every platform below is solid — here's where each one shines, and why churches still pick ChurchGeniusPro.

🗓️

Planning Center

Excellent modular tools, especially Services for worship teams. But each module is priced separately and scales with congregation size — costs add up quickly, and there's no accounting or payroll.

🍃

Breeze

Famously simple at a flat $72/month. Great for basics, but no free plan, no accounting, no worship planning, and the mobile app costs extra as a $119/month bundle.

📒

ChurchTrac

Very affordable with a genuine free plan and built-in accounting. A strong budget pick — but no AI, no payroll, and fewer automation and access-control options.

💸

Tithe.ly ChMS

Strong giving platform with flat pricing. The full experience (app, website, worship tools) needs the $119/month All-Access bundle, and there's no built-in accounting.

Why ChurchGeniusPro?

One platform with members, giving, full accounting with bank sync, W-2 payroll, worship planning, events, and a real AI assistant — starting free and topping out at $45/month, less than half the all-in price of most alternatives.
