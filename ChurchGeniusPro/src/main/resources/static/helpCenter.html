Login page · Sidebar · 🔔 → Signed-in devices
▼

Everyone signs in on the same login page with a username and password. From there you can recover a forgotten password or username, stay signed in on a trusted device, and — if you have more than one linked login — switch between them without signing out.

How to sign in
1Open the ChurchGeniusPro login page and enter your Username and Password (the 👁 button shows or hides the password)
2Tick Remember password only on your own device — it keeps this browser signed in for up to 30 days
3Click Login. Staff go to the Home dashboard, the church owner login goes to Admin → Users, and Member Portal logins go to the Member Portal
If you were signed out in the middle of something, you are taken back to the same page after you sign in again.
After several wrong passwords in a row, sign-in is paused: “Too many unsuccessful login attempts. Please wait and try again later or reset your password.”
How to reset a forgotten password
1On the login page click Forgot Password?
2Enter your Username and the Email address on your account, then click Send Reset Link
3Open the email and use the link within 10 minutes
4Enter a New Password and Confirm Password, then click Reset Password
New passwords need at least 8 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a special character. For a child's Kids Portal login the reset link goes to the head of household's email.
How to recover a forgotten username
1Click Forgot Username?
2Choose 📧 Email or 📱 Phone and enter the address or number linked to your account
3Click Send Username(s) — every username linked to it is sent to you
You can try up to 5 times an hour; after that you are asked to wait an hour.
Automatic log-out after 30 minutes
1Staff logins are signed out after 30 minutes without any activity (clicks, typing or scrolling)
2One minute before, a message asks you to stay: click OK to stay logged in or Cancel to log out now
3If you were signed out, the login page says “You were signed out after a period of inactivity.”
The church owner login can switch this off for a staff login: Admin → Users → 🔐 on the user's row → tick Don't log out automatically → Save Permissions. It is off unless ticked.
Signed-in devices
1Click the 🔔 bell at the top of any page, then 📱 Signed-in devices at the bottom of the panel
2You see every browser or phone that stays signed in for you; the one you are using is marked This device
3Click Sign out on a device, or Sign out of all other devices — those devices need the password next time
Switching between linked accounts
1When your logins have been linked (for example a staff login and your Member Portal login), click your name at the top of the sidebar
2Under Switch Account pick the other login — your current one has a ✓. Go to Home takes you back to your start page
Accounts are linked by the church owner login on the Users page (🔗 Link Accounts) — see Account & User Management.
Common questions
I asked for a reset link but no email came.
The username and email must both match the account, and the church's subscription must be active. For safety the page always shows the same message. Check spam, wait a few minutes (a new link is not sent while the last one is still fresh), then try again.
The reset link says it has expired.
Links last 10 minutes and work once. Request a new one from Forgot Password?.
I don't see my name in the sidebar to switch accounts.
The switch list only appears when you have two or more linked logins. Ask the church owner login to link them.

Trial Accounts

What is different while you are evaluating
▼

A trial is a full working copy of ChurchGeniusPro for a set number of days. Some trials come filled with sample members, families, contributions and events; others start empty. A few things are switched off so that nothing reaches real people or real money while you evaluate.

Trial accounts
Getting started What is limited Test emails Feature tips Request a subscription
Getting started
1On a sample-data trial, the first sign-in shows a Trial Account note with the trial length, start date, end date and days remaining. Click OK to continue — the pages load only after it is accepted
2A sample-data trial shows Your trial portals on the Home page: the staff, Member Portal and Kids Portal sign-ins. Click i to learn what each portal is for
3Explore freely — the sample data is yours to edit or delete
On other trials a small card in the bottom corner shows the end date; in the last 10 days it reappears on each page as a reminder.
What is limited
•Emails never go to members or other real recipients — only one test copy goes to your verified test address (see below)
•Text messages (SMS) are not sent on a trial
•Online giving (Stripe Integration and the Donation Page link) and Activity Corner (Guess It) are not available
•Bank Sync connects to a sandbox (test) bank only
•Password resets, username reminders and verification codes are always delivered
How to receive test emails
1As a SuperAdmin or Admin, open the Email Settings page (link below)
2In 🧪 Trial/Demo Test Email, enter your address and click Send verification code
3Enter the 6-digit code from the email (valid 15 minutes) and click Verify
4From now on each email action — a group email, a meeting notification, a birthday reminder — sends one test copy to that address
Until an address is verified, emails on a trial are not sent at all.
Feature tips
•While you work, small tip cards suggest features you may not have found (for example customizing the dashboard or adding Favorites)
•At most one card at a time and three a day; the page stays usable. Click Got it or ✕ and that tip will not come back
How to request a subscription
1Click Request a subscription → on the trial notice card (also shown when a trial or subscription has ended)
2Check your name and phone, choose a Plan and Billing (Monthly, or Yearly when the plan offers it), add a note if you like
3Click Submit. No payment is taken on this page — the ChurchGeniusPro team contacts you with the next steps
Only the church owner login, a SuperAdmin or an Admin can send the request. An empty trial keeps all its data when it becomes a paid plan; a sample-data trial cannot be converted.
Common questions
I sent a group email but nobody received it.
That is expected on a trial. Verify a test address on Email Settings to see a copy of what would have been sent.
What happens when the trial ends?
Pages stop responding and a message explains the subscription has ended, with a Request a subscription link and a Sign out button.

How to find a family
1Go to Admin → Families. The list shows each family's photo, Primary member, number of Members and Status
2Type in Search by family name… to narrow the list. Click the Primary column heading to sort A–Z or Z–A
3Use Type to choose All Families, Member Families, Non-Member Families, Guests only or Visitors only
4Click the Primary member's name to open the family (read-only view), or the members icon to see everyone in the family in a pop-up
5The list loads a page at a time — click Load More at the bottom to see the rest
A family's type is the Member Type of its Primary member. If the Primary is a Guest or Visitor, the family counts as a non-member family and shows a Guest or Visitor badge next to its status.
The Deleted and Inactive switches show only deleted or inactive families. Turn them off to return to the active list.
How to add a family
1Click ＋ Add Family
2In Primary, enter First Name and Last Name (required), then phone, email, Category (Member, Guest or Visitor), gender, birth month/day/year, address and Nick Name. Click the photo circle to add a picture
3In Family Members, click ＋ Add, fill in the person's details and click ＋ Add Member. Repeat for each person. Tick Same as Primary to copy the Primary's address
4To move someone who is already in your directory, type their name in Search existing members to add to this family… and pick them
5In Roles, choose each person's role: Head of Household, Spouse, Adult or Child. For a Spouse, enter the Anniversary Date
6Click Save Family. A green message confirms the save
If your plan has a maximum number of people, saving is refused once the limit would be passed. The message tells you how many people the plan allows.
How to edit a family
1Click 📝 on the family's row (or open the family and click ✎ Edit Family)
2Change the Primary's details directly. For other people, click Edit on their row, make the change and click ✔ Update Member; click Delete to remove someone from the family
3Click Update Family
The view of a family shows "Membership renewed" with a date when a renewal was applied from Membership Requests.
Privacy and person options
1🔒 Private under Phone, Email or Address hides that detail from members browsing the Member Portal directory. Staff can still see it
2Inactive marks the person inactive. On the Primary, it marks the whole family inactive when you save
3Disable Alerts turns off alerts for that person
4Include in Contributions makes the person selectable in the Contributor field on the Income page
How to search individual members
1On the Families page, click 🔍 Member Search to open it
2Filter by Name, Role, Member Type, Phone or Email, and switch on Show Deleted, Show Inactive or Include in Contributions as needed
3Results show Name, Role, Type, Family, Phone, Email and Status (Active, Inactive or Deleted). Click Clear to start again
A person counts as Deleted or Inactive when they or their family are deleted or inactive. Deleted rows are greyed out and read-only.
Mark inactive, delete and print several families
1Tick the box on each family's row (or the box in the header to select the whole page). Inactive and deleted families cannot be selected
2In the bar that appears, click Mark Inactive, Delete Selected or 📄 Family Report (a printable report of the selected families, Admin and SuperAdmin only)
3Confirm the message
Deleting a family deletes its members too. There is no Restore button on the Families page, so ask your administrator before deleting.
Paper membership forms (PDF and scan)
1On the Add Family page, click 📄 Membership Form (PDF) to download a printable, fillable form with your church's logo
2When it comes back filled in, click 📤 Upload Scanned Form and choose the PDF or picture
3If no matching person is found, click Fill the form for review. If a person with the same email or phone already exists, compare the two and choose Skip Import, Add as New Anyway, Merge Selected or Overwrite Existing
4Check the filled-in fields, then click Save Family (Update Family when you chose Merge Selected or Overwrite Existing)
A filled-in digital PDF reads best. A photo or scan of a paper form can only be read when your church's AI reading is available; otherwise you'll see a message that no details could be read.
Common questions
Where do Guests and Visitors come from?
You can set a person's Category to Guest or Visitor yourself. Every Connect With Us form a visitor submits also creates a new family with that person as a Visitor.
I can't see the 📝 or Delete buttons.
Your login doesn't have that permission. Families has separate checkboxes for Add or Edit, Delete and Mark Inactive. Ask your church account holder to turn the one you need on.
A person is missing from the list.
Clear the search box and set Type to All Families. If they may be inactive or deleted, turn on the Inactive or Deleted switch, or use Member Search with Show Inactive / Show Deleted.
Can a Member Portal login use this page?
Yes, if the church gives that member the Families permission. Members then see families without the details other members marked private.

I don't see 🗑 Delete Request.
