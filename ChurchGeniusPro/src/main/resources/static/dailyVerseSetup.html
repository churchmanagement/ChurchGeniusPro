Daily Verse Setup

Load a full year of verses

Adds one verse for every day of the year to your Daily Verse list, so Include Daily Verse in Email Settings always has something to send. You only ever need to do this once. Verses are stored against a day of the year rather than a date, so the same list is reused every year automatically — there is nothing to reload next January.

Used to work out how many days the year has (a leap year gets a 29 February verse) and which date each day falls on.
