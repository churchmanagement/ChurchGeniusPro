## 1. Human creative contribution

- The complete scene-by-scene script (arrival, greeting, registration form, photo-of-form data entry, sticker printing, sticker layout, AI checkout security dialogue including emergency alerts and automatic follow-ups, classroom walk, goodbye, closing montage, brand end card) was written by Anson before any AI generation. (See section 6.)
- All characters are original fictional characters created for this film. No real person's name, photograph, identity, or likeness was provided to the AI at any point. No celebrity, actor, movie character, or existing fictional character was referenced in any prompt.
- Human editorial work: two takes were generated per scene and a human-directed selection was made (documented per scene below, with reasons); clips were trimmed, ordered, and assembled; the check-in sticker close-up, brand end card, and background music were created outside the AI tool (Python/PIL, ffmpeg, synthesized instrumental) and composited into the final edit.

## 5. Post-production (human work, outside the AI tool)

- Check-in sticker close-up: designed programmatically (Python/PIL) — child name "SAM" top, large check-in code "K-4827", "Classroom A", "Check-In: 9:12 AM" — composited over a blurred frame from the printing scene, animated with a slow zoom (ffmpeg zoompan). Scripts preserved: `make_sticker.py`.
- Brand end card: designed programmatically per ChurchGeniusPro house style (purple gradient, logo card, tagline, CTA pill, URL). Script preserved: `make_endcard.py`.
- Music: original instrumental synthesized from scratch in Python/numpy (Am–F–C–G pads, pluck arpeggio, soft percussion, ~112 BPM), mixed at low level under dialogue. Script preserved: `make_music.py`.
- Edit: clips trimmed to dialogue timing (measured via transcription), upscaled 720×1280→1080×1920, concatenated with the insert and end card, mixed, and encoded with ffmpeg. Final runtime 90.0s.
